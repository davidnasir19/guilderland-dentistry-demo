Meet Dr. Park

Skilled hands.
A kind manner.

A personal reason to care.
A thoughtful way to practice.

Dr. Park's own dental experiences as a child inspired her to become a dentist. Today, she brings that perspective to family and cosmetic care, with a belief that education and understanding are part of a healthier smile.

She earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery, cum laude, at the University at Buffalo, and completed a residency in Advanced Education in General Dentistry.

More about Dr. Park

Dr. Park also served as an Adjunct Clinical Professor at the University at Buffalo School of Dental Medicine from 2012 to 2013. Outside the practice, she volunteers at food banks and her local church.

Professional memberships listed by the practice include the American Dental Association, Academy of General Dentistry, New York State Dental Association and Third District Dental Society.

Before you visit

A few things
you might wonder.

Something else on your mind?
Give us a call. We're here to help.

How do I book an appointment?

Call 518–356–0077 during office hours. Our team can help you find a time and answer questions about your visit.

What should I do before my first visit?

Please complete your online medical history form and arrive about 15 minutes early so we can review it with you.

Do you see children and families?

Yes. Dr. Park provides family dentistry for patients of all ages. Call us to talk about appointments for you or your children.

What about insurance and payment?

Please call our team before your visit to confirm your insurance details and discuss available payment arrangements.

Is the office accessible?

The practice is listed as having a wheelchair-accessible entrance, parking and restroom. Please call if you need help planning your arrival.
